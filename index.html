The "Cashback Trap"

You have SBI Cashback, Amazon Pay ICICI, HSBC Live+. These are excellent "Cashback" cards (saving 5-10%).

However: They do NOT earn miles. To fly Business Class to Europe, you need points that transfer to Airlines. You are currently earning "fixed value" (₹1 = ₹1) instead of "potential value" (1 Mile = ₹1 to ₹4).
